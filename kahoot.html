Kahoot

Four quizzes and three polls on the phones. Every question, with the answers of the room.

The seven games in the order of the two days. Choose a game. Each chart shows all answer options of one question and how many players chose each one. In the quizzes the correct answer has a red bar. The polls have no correct answer.

Day 1
Day 2

room

The room was young and mixed: 20 of 29 were under 35, and women and men were almost equal in number (16 and 17). Most participants were engineers from utilities and private companies, and 18 of 31 work mainly on generation.

IPP: independent power producer. EPC: engineering, procurement and construction. NGO: non-governmental organisation. KenGen: Kenya Electricity Generating Company. KETRACO: Kenya Electricity Transmission Company. REREC: Rural Electrification and Renewable Energy Corporation.

q1

The room knew where the imports come from: 24 of 35 named Ethiopia. The exact figures were less known. A discussion about planning needs shared numbers, so the session started from the key numbers, each with its source.

FY: financial year. HFO: heavy fuel oil. INEP: Integrated National Energy Plan. LCPDP: Least Cost Power Development Plan.

q2

Ideas with a clear picture landed. Of 30 answers, 20 identified the end-use method, and 19 of 27 knew what a loss of load expectation of 3 hours a year means. Questions that ask for a transfer were harder.

GDP: gross domestic product. LCPDP: Least Cost Power Development Plan. LCOE: levelised cost of electricity. MILP: mixed-integer linear program.

power_at_home

Blackouts are part of daily life for this group: 22 of 27 had two or more in the last year. The game gave points for speed, so the answers show a tendency and not a survey result.

PV: photovoltaics.

poll

The participants live with frequent power cuts. Of 32 answers, 23 report two or more cuts in the last month. Most have no backup: 21 of 32 wait until the power returns. The group is small and does not represent Kenyan households.

LPG: liquefied petroleum gas. KES: Kenyan shilling.

q3

The participants know how their system runs over the day. Of 30 answers, 25 named what covers the evening peak, and 20 of 26 knew that spare geothermal steam is vented. Yearly shares and comparisons with other countries were less known.

FY: financial year. PV: photovoltaics.

q4

The vocabulary of models did not settle in one afternoon. Questions 5, 8 and 9 build on the lab exercise of the follow-up material, so they go beyond what the session covered. The best result was the power flow question: 16 of 30 knew that the reactance decides how the flow splits.

LCPDP: Least Cost Power Development Plan. O and M: operation and maintenance. DC: direct current.

The results rest on 20 to 35 answers per question. They describe this group and should not be generalised. A question runs for 20 or 30 seconds, so a low share of correct answers reflects time pressure as well as knowledge. One player is one phone.

Tools

The lab, the Miro board and the web apps of the training, and free tools to start with.

Kenya Power Planning Lab

A teaching lab in one web page, open source, by Lampros Konstantellos. It runs online and offline. All numbers in the lab are rounded and meant for training, not for decisions.

Build Kenya's 2035 mix

Reach 5,560 MW of firm capacity at the evening peak and cover 31,135 GWh, within USD 10 billion. Seven options are available. Three shock cards then test the plan, and a small least-cost model shows its own answer.

Run the grid for a day

Act as the control room for one day. Move the day's hydro water, add a battery and shift evening demand. The lab shows the cost of the day and the cost of the next MWh in each hour.

The web apps of Tim Lorenz

Both apps ran in groups of two to four on one phone or laptop per group.

  • Investment Simulation

    A web game in three phases. Each group invests USD 200 million in solar, wind and geothermal plants in Kenya and follows the cash flow to 2055.

    Open the Investment Simulation

  • Household energy lab

    The groups build a demand profile, guess the sizes of photovoltaics (PV) and battery, simulate them and compare them with the cost optimum. The optimisation behind it is urbs.

    Open the household energy lab

The two apps ran on a temporary web address during the workshop. A link appears here when an app has a permanent address.
